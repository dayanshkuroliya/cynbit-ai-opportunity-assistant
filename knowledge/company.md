# Cynbit Technologies

## Company Overview

Cynbit Technologies is a technology company based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, focused on AI automation, custom software development, scalable business platforms, cloud infrastructure, and digital growth solutions.

Cynbit describes its focus around autonomous AI agents, custom software development, full-stack development, cloud systems, and digital marketing/SEO.
